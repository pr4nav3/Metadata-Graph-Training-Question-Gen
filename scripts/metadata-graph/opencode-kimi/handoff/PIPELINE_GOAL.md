The goal of the Kimi + OpenCode SEBI pipeline is to train a model to remember the SEBI document corpus: facts, rules, dates, actors, exceptions, procedures, document lineages, and practical relationships across as much of the corpus as possible. We generate questions because hard, evidence-grounded, multi-document QAs are the training signal for that memory; coverage and memory density matter more than ornamental novelty, while uniqueness only prevents duplicate or same-intent rows that would waste training budget. Every stage should therefore prefer useful, difficult questions that teach distinct SEBI-document memory targets and expand corpus coverage, not questions that are merely unusual, over-curated, or different for difference's sake.
